### Summary

Scope: the four findings in `2026-10-11-zig-ui-atlas-design-review.md` and direct regressions introduced by their fixes. Reviewed specification SHA-256: `c6e54e44899c8684605da612316e26a5d12a4d58acc14b405618793b74a4d55e`. The original review is preserved unchanged.

### Issue 1: Candidate-only publication has no successful outcome

- **Severity**: major
- **Section**: Transactional publication and check mode, lines 177–194; Proposed exact atlas gate, line 232
- **Description**: Step 2 explicitly says “For candidate-only generation no receipt exists and selection is never touched.” However, `CommittedDurable` requires “Replacement and final selection-parent durability barrier completed,” and CLI exit 0 permits publication “only for `CommittedDurable`.” The other outcomes describe failure/cancellation or an indeterminate selection replacement. A successfully written, renamed and synced qualification candidate therefore cannot return any success outcome without falsely claiming a selection commit. This directly breaks the prescribed pre-selection `generate` → `check-candidate` flow and conflicts with candidate generation remaining available independently of release promotion. The same missing distinction leaves ambiguous how a candidate-only directory-rename durability failure should be reported without implying a selected pointer changed.
- **Suggestion**: Separate candidate artifact publication from selection promotion in the result model. Define successful durable candidate publication after step 3, including exact existing-candidate/idempotent success, with selection explicitly untouched; allow exit 0 for that outcome on `generate`. Keep `CommittedDurable` and the current selection commit classifications for promotion. Define candidate failure/indeterminate durability diagnostics and ownership-preserving cleanup without pretending that any release selection was attempted. Add focused fixtures for fresh candidate success, idempotent candidate success, post-candidate-rename sync failure, and no selection/receipt creation; retain the existing promotion commit and cancellation fixtures.
- **Status**: open

### Strengths

- The durability correction distinguishes visibility from persistence and explicitly refuses unsupported release promotion. Linux directory sync, macOS power-loss flush qualification and Windows's unproven directory durability remain requirements, not claims that actual platforms passed. No truncate/write or file-flush-only fallback silently reports success.
- Source binding covers ordinary stale executables and compilation/source races without embedding platform-specific executable hashes into reproducible payloads. Its stated trust boundary is the independently trusted repository build adapter/receipt and approved toolchain. A caller-supplied receipt is not made trusted merely by hashing it; implementation must preserve the stated independent build provenance and forged-receipt rejection requirement. This review adds no remote signing or compromised-OS defense requirement.
- New raw arithmetic fixtures constrain implementers before product raster goldens exist, while the fully owned Zig/no-foreign-raster requirement and pending actual visual qualification remain intact.
