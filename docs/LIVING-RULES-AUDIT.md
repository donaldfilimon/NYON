# Independent Living rules audit — 2026-10-01

Read-only audit of the approved rules against the current economy, diplomacy and fleet candidate source during the artifact freeze. This is source evidence and proposed regression work, not an executed full gameplay qualification. Whole-boundary work budgeting remains a separate Partial requirement.

## Concrete corrections

1. **Current source defect: exploration selects a different scout from the funded eligibility witness.** `rules.rs::reserve_exploration` accepts a mixed fleet when any scout can launch, using that hull's return-credit cost; it subsequently splits the first scout by stable ID without applying the same funded predicate. A lower-ID uncredited scout costs four energy while a higher-ID credited scout costs two. With two energy, the fleet passes eligibility but reserves the uncredited scout, despite the credited scout being actionable. Minimal correction: choose the exact funded scout before the split and preserve that witness. Regression: two mixed scouts, energy two, reachable unseen world; reserve the credited scout and depart no earlier than the next boundary at the correct cost.

2. **Current source defect: war eligibility counts an empire-wide total as an assembled fleet.** `rules.rs::assembled_attack` sums escorts in every owned docked fleet, including busy/reserved fleets and fleets unable to reach the target. `war_target` uses that global strength for the target threshold. Approved rules section 6 requires an assembled eligible escort fleet and excludes queued/distant assets. Reduced case: three disconnected owned colonies with one escort each, a rival battery at a world adjacent to only the first colony; global strength thirty passes against defense twenty, while the sole reachable fleet strength ten fails. Minimal correction: evaluate the maximum eligible single assembled fleet for each target, including idle/reservation/funded-launch/reachability constraints. Additional distant/busy fleets must not change the decision. The rules do not define a numerical distance cutoff; no new cutoff is inferred by this review.

3. **Partial missing normative candidate: existing useful routes cannot be adjusted.** `reserve_economy_proposals` suppresses supply whenever a route already has the same source/destination/resource/source owner, including suspended or mismatched batch/reserve/receiver/kind records. Economic application always creates a new route. Approved section 7 explicitly requires establishing or adjusting a useful direct internal route, with bilateral permission for trade and restricted Trader aid. Reduced case: source energy thirty, owned destination energy zero with known scheduled demand, existing suspended internal route with batch one and reserve nine thousand; the useful affordable adjustment is never proposed. Minimal correction: reserve an exact existing route adjustment from the shared snapshot, preserve its due clock, enforce current owners and permission, and keep cadence ten. Regression: suspended/mismatched parameters recover; captured source or stale permission rejects without state/resource mutation; existing cargo remains immutable.

These findings were sent to root and authority. Source corrections and their tests are owned by authority. A proposed additive `Route` decision payload may append omitted-when-None `existing_route`, preserving existing establishment bytes; no source/vector change is claimed by this audit.
