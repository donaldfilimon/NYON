## Selected requirement and proposed realization

Strict imminent Food/energy recovery over discretionary research, expansion and military spending is already selected. Its proposed realization uses a 200-boundary counterfactual, a first-needs deadline within 100 ticks, and four admission tiers: imminent needs recovery, recovery, obligation, ordinary. For ordinary owned-needs category 0, a candidate must strictly reduce FirstExposure or ImminentLoss within 100 ticks, must not increase combined FirstExposure for any baseline threatened owned colony, and must preserve each already-funded first due payment in that interval. Later-only relief is category 1. A preparatory Food/energy route or offer may qualify for category 0 through an exact live public request even without predicted delivery; that exception cannot sacrifice the supplier’s protected needs. Due Food/energy is held before discretionary recipe/lab/repair/military supply debits. A protected-energy Food recipe is allowed only when its atomic result improves combined exposure without breaking either funded resource. Public-request coverage is an opportunity, never promised stock or predicted delivery.
